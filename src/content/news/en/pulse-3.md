We are releasing **Pulse 3.0**, the third version of the platform we use to operate 1.1 GW of our own and third-party plants.

## What is new

- Predictive models that anticipate inverter failures up to **21 days** ahead
- Native drone-thermography integration: every hotspot is geolocated on the plant map
- Owner reports that combine technical and financial data in one view

Pulse integrates with Modbus, OPC-UA and the main manufacturers' APIs, and is available for plants not built by GEOLight.
