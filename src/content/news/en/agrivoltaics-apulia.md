One year after start-up, the **Tavoliere** agrivoltaic project (Foggia, Italy) has released its first agronomic data, measured with the University of Foggia.

## First-year results

- **−22%** soil evaporation under the structures
- Durum wheat yield on par with the control plot
- Peak crop temperature 3.1 °C lower in July

Structures raised to 4.5 metres let farm machinery work as usual. The farmer keeps the harvest and earns a stable lease for 30 years.
