GEOLight has started building its first **hybrid** project: 40 MWh of LFP batteries added to the 90 MWp Guadiana photovoltaic plant in Badajoz, Spain.

## Why hybridise

Solar noon coincides with the lowest market prices. Batteries store part of that energy and sell it into the evening peak. Our models point to an **18% increase in revenue per MWh**.

## In-house EMS

The system will run on the EMS built by GEOLight's digital team, which optimises the strategy across day-ahead, intraday and balancing markets every 15 minutes.

Commercial operation is expected in the first quarter of 2027.
